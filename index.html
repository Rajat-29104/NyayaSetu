Legal Guidance
How to navigate India's legal system and access free legal aid.
📋 How to File an FIR — Step by Step
1
Police Station
Go to station in whose jurisdiction the incident occurred
2
Ask SHO
Register FIR — FREE, your legal right under Sec. 154 CrPC
3
If Refused
Approach SP/DCP or file with Magistrate under Sec. 156(3) CrPC
4
Get Copy
Always collect FREE copy. Note FIR number.
🏛️ NALSA — Free Legal Aid
Call 15100 or visit nalsa.gov.in
✅ Who's Eligible?
SC/ST, women, children, PwD, income < ₹1 lakh/year.
⚖️ Lok Adalat
Disputes settled free of court fees. Final and binding award.

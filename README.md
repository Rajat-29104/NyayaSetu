# 🇮🇳 NyayaSetu — AI Civic & Legal Empowerment

> ### Know Your Rights. Access What's Yours.

**NyayaSetu** is an AI-powered civic-tech platform designed to make government schemes, legal rights, RTI procedures, grievance channels, and citizen assistance easier to discover, understand, and act upon.

The core idea is simple:

> **Citizens should not need to understand bureaucratic complexity before they can access the systems designed for them.**

NyayaSetu acts as a bridge between a citizen's problem and the appropriate civic information, process, or official channel.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Government information and citizen services are often available, but difficult to navigate.

A citizen may know that they need help but still not know:

- Which government scheme they may be eligible for
- What rights apply to their situation
- How to frame an RTI request
- Where to submit a grievance
- Which documents may be required
- What a scholarship status means
- Which helpline or official portal to use
- What practical step to take next

The problem is therefore not simply **lack of information**.

The bigger challenge is the gap between:

**Information → Understanding → Action**

NyayaSetu addresses this gap by bringing multiple citizen-facing journeys into a single, guided interface.
